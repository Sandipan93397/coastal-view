Essential Guest Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, upfront answers regarding location, check-in, payment policies, dining, and travel arrangements.

Coastal View is located at Rina Rd, Mandarmani, West Bengal 721455, directly on the beachfront. It is approximately 170 km from Kolkata, which takes around 3.5 to 4 hours by road via NH16 and NH116B through Kolaghat and Nandakumar to Chawalkhola.

Our standard check-in time is 12:00 PM (noon) and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to room availability upon request with our front desk.

To confirm your reservation, a 20% advance payment is required at the time of booking. The remaining 80% balance is payable at the hotel upon check-in or check-out. Please note: The 20% advance is non-refundable.

Breakfast is not included in the room rate, but you can add it for ₹200 when booking. It's served 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM.

The beachfront infinity pool is open daily from 7:00 AM to 7:30 PM (proper swimwear required). The Bay Breeze Restaurant serves breakfast from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM, lunch from 12:30 PM to 3:30 PM, and dinner from 7:30 PM to 10:45 PM. Room service is available 24/7.

Yes, absolutely! We offer romantic Beachside Canopy dinners (₹3,500) and Infinity Poolside setups (₹2,800), including a 4-course chef tasting menu, private butler, floral decor, celebration cake, and sparkling mocktails. You can add this directly to your booking engine reservation.

Our founder Anirban Das operates Ani's Tour and Travels. You can toggle "Need a car?" in our booking engine for daily car hire (₹2,500/day) or visit our tours link (Ani's Tour Enquiry) for customized Kolkata pickups and Tajpur/Digha day trips.

Mandarmani is pleasant year-round. October to March offers crisp sea breezes, pleasant sunshine, and cool evenings. The monsoon months (July to September) offer dramatic cloudy seascapes and roaring high tides cherished by nature lovers.

Coastal View warmly welcomes couples and families. All guests above 18 years must provide valid government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License) upon check-in as per government hotel regulations.
